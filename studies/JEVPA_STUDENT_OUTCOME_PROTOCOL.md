# Pre-registration: diversity-gated JEVPA on student outcomes

## Question

This third real-data study asks whether diversity-gated Pareto retention can produce a qualifying, held-out improvement over greedy search when an input record contains distinct academic and financial evidence. It follows two real-data null studies in which proposed heads were near duplicates.

## Source, task, and blindness

Use UCI's *Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success* CSV (dataset 697), downloaded from the UCI release and checksum-recorded before the split. Retain only rows whose target is `Dropout` or `Graduate`; exclude all `Enrolled` rows before sampling. The decision task is: from the information recorded by the end of the second semester, classify the eventual outcome as `dropout` or `graduate`. This is a historical public-data research task, not a deployment recommendation.

Render each row into a stable, human-readable student record. The text includes academic progress, enrollment/background, and financial-status values and never includes the target. A prior local feasibility screen used the deterministic 40% evaluation partition generated by `numpy.random.default_rng(20260928)` after filtering the binary cohort. Reconstruct and exclude every row in that partition before sampling this study. From the remaining rows, freeze a deterministic source sample and its ID manifest using seed `20260929`: 500 discovery rows, 600 selection rows, and 800 test rows. The analyst sees only discovery texts and their binary labels. It never sees selection/test labels, IDs, slice membership, or model summaries derived from them. Fitting uses discovery labels only.

The selection and test labels are sealed until their registered uses. No selection result may affect candidate fitting. The held-out test is scored only when a qualifying JEVPA-only merge wins selection; otherwise no test Jev request is made.

## Sealed selection slices

Before sampling, define two text-independent record predicates:

* **Academic underperformance:** `Curricular units 2nd sem (approved)` is less than half of `Curricular units 2nd sem (enrolled)`, or `Curricular units 2nd sem (grade)` is below 10.
* **Financial strain without academic underperformance:** (`Debtor` is 1 or `Tuition fees up to date` is 0) and the academic-underperformance predicate is false.

The selection set reserves 200 academic-underperformance rows and 150 financial-strain rows, then fills the remaining 250 rows from the seeded remainder. The lower financial reservation reflects reconstruction and exclusion of the prior feasibility-screen evaluation partition before any live request. Each slice must contain at least 50 selection labels before it participates in retention. The test set is not slice-balanced and remains untouched unless the selection gate passes.

## Search, comparison, and gates

Begin with one holistic question: whether the supplied student record more likely describes eventual dropout or graduation. Four shared analyst calls, each with exactly one additive, text-answerable element, begin from the same base scorecard and discovery evidence. Retirements and rewordings are invalid. All valid shared candidates receive the same Jev answers in one batch over the 500 discovery and 600 selection rows.

Greedy selects minimum overall selection Brier. JEVPA retains at most four nondominated candidates over overall, academic-underperformance, and financial-strain Brier. It may merge two candidates only when they uniquely win different sealed slices and their selection prediction vectors have Pearson correlation below 0.95 or hard-class disagreement of at least 10%. Each arm has two further analyst calls; all candidate fits use the identical trusted discovery labels, full feature coverage, inverse-propensity weighting, and out-of-fold calibration. The model never supplies weights or calibration.

The primary outcome is paired test Brier, JEVPA minus greedy. A practically positive study requires a JEVPA-only qualifying merge, Brier improvement of at least 0.005, and no accuracy loss greater than 0.02. If the merge fails its selection or diversity gate, stop before test scoring and report the mechanism as not activated.

## Budget and records

The request ceiling is 4,600 Jev item requests: 500 base, 1,100 shared initial, 2,200 branch, and 800 final. Record the planned and actual token/cost usage for every batch, complete analyst replies, scorecards, cache coverage, frozen manifest checksum, candidate lineages, candidate metrics, and all stopping decisions. Do not modify this protocol after any analyst or Jev request.
